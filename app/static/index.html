      How it works

      1. Establishment records for the configured boroughs and SCIAN classes are downloaded from the official DENUE API and reduced to the minimum fields needed.
      2. Exact counts by borough, category and employment size are computed in Python during ingestion and re-verified at startup.
      3. Short factual descriptions of aggregates and a sample of establishments are embedded locally with FastEmbed.
      4. Each question is parsed for boroughs, categories and intent; counts, percentages, rankings and comparisons come straight from the calculations.
      5. Semantic retrieval adds supporting facts and handles open questions.
      6. A language model, when enabled, only rephrases the verified result; answers that introduce new numbers are discarded.
      7. Every answer shows its filters, metrics, methodology and the INEGI source.

      Scope and limitations

      • Only the listed boroughs and retail categories are included.
      • Records are a snapshot from the retrieval date shown above.
      • Establishment names appear only in example answers; contact details and street addresses are never stored.
      • Results are analytical transformations of public data, not official INEGI statistics.
