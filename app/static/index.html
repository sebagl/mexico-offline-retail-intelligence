      Categories map to SCIAN 2023 classes; the official Spanish class titles are shown under each name. Coverage is limited to the configured boroughs and categories — it is not a citywide or national view.

      How it works
      1. Establishment records for the configured boroughs and SCIAN classes are downloaded from the official DENUE API and reduced to the minimum fields needed — no contact details, addresses or coordinates.
      2. Exact counts by borough, category and employment size are computed in Python during ingestion and re-verified at startup.
      3. Each question is parsed for boroughs, categories and intent; counts, percentages, rankings and comparisons come straight from those calculations.
      4. Short factual descriptions are embedded locally to supply supporting evidence and handle open questions.
      5. When enabled, a language model only rephrases the verified result; any reply that changes or adds a number is discarded.
      Scope and limitations
      • Only the listed boroughs and retail categories are included; results describe this selection, not Mexico City as a whole.
      • Records are a snapshot from the retrieval date shown above.
      • Counts describe registered establishments only; they say nothing about sales, size of demand or market potential.
      • Establishment names appear only in example answers.
